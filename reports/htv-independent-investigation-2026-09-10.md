# HTV Network: independent investigation beyond the CBC video

Research date: **10 September 2026**. This supplements the [original video analysis](/Users/malickdes/AIWORKSPACE/MoneyPrinterTurbo/reports/htv-network-campaign-research-2026-09-10.md).

Independent sources strengthen three parts of the picture: HTV publicly recruits for a multilingual news-and-Facebook workflow; older fact-checks document fabricated stories distributed through Autulu and Btuatu; and current website code connects several additional hosts through HTV branding and advertising configuration. **These findings do not establish that HTV owns every account or domain discussed below.**

## 1. What the operating strategy appears to be

The evidence supports a working model of audience-specific Facebook distribution feeding advertising-supported websites. Recruitment documents describe the workflow; archived posts and fact-checks show examples of its broader distribution pattern; current website configuration supplies separate infrastructure evidence. Attribution must be assessed for each connection.

| Component | Evidence | What it supports |
|---|---|---|
| Foreign-market publishing | HTV advertises English/Spanish news writing for the Americas and Europe, trend monitoring, use of its existing publishing system, and management of assigned fanpages. | An organized production-and-distribution operation, as described by the employer. [Recruitment listing](https://www.topcv.vn/viec-lam/content-marketing-khong-yeu-cau-kinh-nghiem/2087830.html) |
| Repeatable celebrity narratives | March 2025 fact-checks identified similar invented Elon Musk donation-rejection stories with different sports figures on Autulu and Btuatu. | Reuse of a narrative across personalities and domains; this alone does not identify the author or software. [Autulu example](https://leadstories.com/hoax-alert/2025/03/fact-check-patrick-mahomes-shocks-the-world-by-rejecting-elon-musks-10-million-offer-keep-it-and-spe.html), [Btuatu example](https://leadstories.com/hoax-alert/2025/03/fact-check-dawn-staley-elon-musk-10-million.html) |
| Fan-page distribution | An archived Basketball Lovers post contains a Btuatu URL; two publishers document Kelce Fan Club Family distributing material associated with Btuatu and Autulu. | Observable distribution relationships, independent of the CBC video. See the account inventory below. |
| Advertising monetization setup | Three currently retrieved hosts embed the same Google advertising publisher identifier; two also share analytics and ad-slot identifiers. | Shared configuration or an advertising-management relationship. It does not measure revenue. See the website inventory below. |
| Expansion beyond celebrity news | Two Long Bien Truck subdomains currently serve elaborate English first-person family dramas. | The inspected content includes emotional personal storytelling. Factuality and AI authorship of these samples were not established. [us115](https://us115.longbientruck.com/), [us23](https://us23.longbientruck.com/) |

My inference is that reusable narratives and topic-specific pages make the publishing process easier to repeat across audiences, while external websites provide advertising inventory. The evidence does **not** establish a centrally planned political campaign, a particular AI model, paid promotion by these exact accounts, or campaign profitability.

## 5. Corporate and recruitment evidence

HTV's [TopCV employer profile](https://www.topcv.vn/cong-ty/cong-ty-co-phan-htv-network/178856.html) identifies **tax ID 0110654781**, a Hanoi business, and a self-reported staff band of **100–499**. This is a useful identity anchor; the headcount is not independently audited.

The content listing offers **6–8 million VND base salary plus article fees**, advertising **12–20 million VND total monthly income**. It asks for basic English, requires no prior experience, and offers writing training. It displayed ten openings and a **21 September 2026** deadline when checked. The ad does not name its websites or instruct applicants to fabricate news; advertised compensation is not verified payroll. [Primary job listing](https://www.topcv.vn/viec-lam/content-marketing-khong-yeu-cau-kinh-nghiem/2087830.html).

A separate accounting vacancy asks for marketing-cost controls and budget reporting. That supports the existence of advertised administrative functions, without establishing spending or revenue. [Accounting listing](https://www.topcv.vn/viec-lam/ke-toan-tong-hop-lam-t2-t6-thu-viec-100-luong/2149732.html).

Registry mirrors associate the HTV tax ID with **19 March 2024**, advertising, and representative **Ngô Anh Tân**, but disagree on company form and capital. No authoritative registry certificate was recovered, so the current legal form and capital remain unsettled. ## 6. Boundaries that affect the conclusion

- **Other Vietnamese operators are distinct leads.** A September 2025 Nieuwscheckers investigation mapped 254 Dutch/Flemish pages and identified Beeup in that ecosystem. It does not establish Beeup equals HTV. The broader MEO network and CBC's HTV subset also must not be combined into one independently verified inventory. [Nieuwscheckers investigation](https://nieuwscheckers.nl/vietnamese-pulpproducenten-verdienen-via-facebook-aan-massameuk-over-misdaad-sport-en-soap/).
- **Shared infrastructure does not settle legal ownership.** Reused branding, an ad publisher ID, analytics and matching configuration collectively strengthen a connection; an agency or service provider could still explain parts of it.
- **Current failures are not takedown evidence.** Several homepages returned Cloudflare, TLS or DNS errors from this research environment. Search indexes sometimes retained readable content. Neither observation establishes a global shutdown or uninterrupted operation.
- **Important unknowns remain:** the complete current page inventory, verified audience totals, paid-ad activity, revenue, beneficial ownership of each domain, exact AI tools, and the legal entity represented by the text `htvnetworkllc`.
- CBC's blocked original website was not accessed through a workaround. This follow-up relies on other public sources and current site observations, with no login or contact with the subjects.
